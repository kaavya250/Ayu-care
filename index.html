About AyuCare

Tradition-inspired. Clear about its limits.

Good health information should be accessible, transparent and careful about what it can and cannot tell you.

What this site does

It organises your supplied health-topic dataset, makes it searchable, and displays source descriptions, remedy text and yoga names with clear safety labels. Duplicate entries are merged for display while their distinct notes are retained.

What this site does not do

It is not a medical device, diagnostic tool, emergency service or substitute for a doctor. The uploaded dataset has not been clinically validated here. Do not stop prescribed treatment or start herbs, supplements or home remedies based on this website.
